# List of Sub-processors

## Annex IV – List of sub-processors

This page is Annex IV of the Data Processing Agreement for Autopilot Monitor. Under Clause 7.7(1), the controller has given its general authorisation for the engagement of the sub-processors listed below.

The list covers all sub-processors engaged by glueckkanja AG. Not every sub-processor is involved in every processing operation; the column "Description of processing" states the role of each. For Autopilot Monitor, data of the controller is processed by Microsoft (the platform itself), Freshworks (support tickets, including data the controller submits with them) and Mailchimp (onboarding and offboarding e-mails to the controller's administrators). The technical picture is described on Data Flows & External Services.

## Changes to this list

Additions to or replacements of sub-processors are announced under Service Announcements at least 30 days before the sub-processor is engaged. The announcement is the notification under Clause 7.7(1) of the Data Processing Agreement. The controller may object within that period via the [support form](https://products.glueckkanja.com/support/tickets/new?ticket_form=drop_a_question_\(autopilotmonitor\)); see Version and changes in the [Data Processing Agreement](./).
