## Changes to this list

Additions to or replacements of sub-processors are announced under Service Announcements at least 30 days before the sub-processor is engaged. The announcement is the notification under Clause 7.7(1) of the Data Processing Agreement. The controller may object within that period via the [support form](https://products.glueckkanja.com/support/tickets/new?ticket_form=drop_a_question_\(autopilotmonitor\)); see Version and changes in the Data Processing Agreement.
