## Plans and license metric

Autopilot Monitor is available in two [plans](../plans.md):&#x20;

* **Autopilot Monitor Community Edition,**&#x20;
* **Autopilot Monitor Pro**.

Both plans are licensed **per Microsoft 365 tenant**. There are no license limits based on devices, enrollments, or users.

## Pro trial

* A Pro trial gives your Community Edition tenant the complete Pro feature set, including up to 2 delegated tenants, free of charge. It runs on your existing tenant; existing data is preserved.
* Available on request via the portal.
* No support entitlement and no service level.
* When the trial ends, the tenant falls back to Community Edition automatically. The rules under End of Pro apply.

## Delegated tenants

With Pro you can manage other tenants from your own tenant, the **primary tenant**. This is intended for managed service providers managing their customers' tenants and for organizations managing their affiliates' or additional tenants.

### Included and add-on

* **Up to 2 delegated tenants** are included in the Pro subscription fee.
* Additional delegated tenants are subscribed as an **add-on**, priced per delegated tenant. The add-on term is coupled to the primary subscription: add-ons can be added during the term and removed at renewal.
* Delegated tenants receive the Pro feature set. A tenant that already uses Community Edition receives the Pro feature set as soon as it becomes a delegated tenant.

### Contracting party and consent

Our only contracting party is the subscriber of the primary tenant. The delegated tenant grants admin consent in the portal; it does not enter into a separate agreement with us.

### Limits

* MCP rate limits and the AI quota are **shared** across the primary tenant and all its delegated tenants.

### Support and service level

Only the subscriber of the primary tenant is entitled to support and can open tickets. Incidents in delegated tenants are covered when the subscriber reports them. The availability target and time credits under Support & Service Level accrue to the subscriber only; delegated tenants have no claims of their own.

### No nesting

A delegated tenant cannot have delegated tenants of its own.

## Subscription, renewal and cancellation

* Pro is available as a **monthly** or **annual** subscription.
* Pro is transacted through **Microsoft Marketplace** or **Cleverbridge**. It may also be available through authorized partners.
* Auto-renewal is the default on both platforms; you can disable it. You can cancel at any time during the current term; the cancellation takes effect at the end of that term.
* Renewal and cancellation mechanics (cut-off times, notice handling, invoicing, payment methods, currency) are governed by the terms of the transaction platform you use.
* **Non-profit organizations** may request special conditions.
* If you are in default of payment, we may stop the Pro services **immediately**; the timing is at our discretion. The rules under End of Pro then apply.

## Changing plans and end of Pro

### Upgrade to Pro

An upgrade from Community Edition to Pro takes place on the same tenant. Existing data is preserved.

### End of Pro

Pro ends when your subscription ends by cancellation or non-renewal, when a Pro trial ends, or when we stop the Pro services because of payment default. In all three cases:

* The tenant falls back to Community Edition.
* Pro-only features and settings are disabled automatically.
* **Data older than 90 days is deleted.** Data within the Community Edition retention period is kept.
* Delegation is revoked. Each delegated tenant falls back to Community Edition individually, and the same data rule applies to each of them. You lose access to the delegated tenants.

## Restrictions on use

For all plans, you must not:

* resell the service or make it available to third parties other than through the delegated tenant model
* circumvent limits, quotas, or plan restrictions by technical or organizational means
* use the service to build a competing product

If you breach one of these rules, we may **suspend or terminate the affected tenant immediately**, at our discretion.
