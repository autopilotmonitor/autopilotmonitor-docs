# How to purchase

Autopilot Monitor Pro is sold through two channels. Both lead to the same Pro plan on your existing tenant — your data stays where it is. Pick the one that fits how your organization buys software:

* **Microsoft Marketplace** — subscribe through the Microsoft commercial marketplace and pay through your Azure billing. [Open the listing](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/saas/glueckkanja-gabag.autopilot-monitor-transactable-prod?tab=Overview) · [Purchase guide](microsoft-marketplace.md)
* **Cleverbridge** — online checkout by credit card, PayPal or bank transfer. [Go to checkout](https://www.cleverbridge.com/306/purl-Autopilot-Monitor-Buy-Y) · [Purchase guide](cleverbridge.md)

What Pro adds is listed under [Plans](../../plans.md). To try Pro first, a tenant administrator can start a one-time, free 30-day trial in the portal under **Settings → Tenant → Plan**.
